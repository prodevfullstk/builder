# Grilling: Relentless Requirements Alignment

Interview the user in structured rounds to eliminate assumptions and clarify ambiguous requirements before building.

## 1. The Design Tree
- Every product decision branches into dependent decisions.
- Formulate high-leverage questions that resolve the root architectural decisions first.

## 2. Round-Based Questions
When requirements are vague, present questions in structured rounds:
- **Q1**: Specific question with clear multiple-choice options.
- **Recommendation**: Provide the best technical recommendation clearly.

## 3. Scope & Constraints
- Clarify target audience, core features, auth requirements, and theme preferences.
- Once requirements are confirmed, immediately transition to building without asking redundant questions.
